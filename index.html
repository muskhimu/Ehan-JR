// About

Research first. Hype never.

I spend my time inside Web3: reading whitepapers, testing new protocols, and tracking which projects are actually worth a user's time or capital. My work sits at the intersection of research and practical guidance — turning a noisy, fast-moving space into a short, clear list of things worth doing.

I guide people through both ends of the spectrum: free entry points that only cost time and attention, and paid opportunities that need capital or deeper commitment. Every recommendation is filtered through the same lens — is this legitimate, and is it worth it right now.
